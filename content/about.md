DevOps and platform engineer based in Taipei, Taiwan. I build CI/CD pipelines, automate infrastructure, and operate Kubernetes-based platforms on bare metal and private cloud. I write tooling in Go and Python.

## Currently

I work as a Cloud Infrastructure Engineer at Techmeister. I evaluate cloud connectivity and architecture for telecommunications clients, including GCP Dedicated Interconnect, HA VPN, WireGuard on GCP Compute Engine, and multi-cloud migration constraints.

## Previously

I was an Infrastructure Engineer and Graduate Researcher at the O-RAN Software Community Asia Pacific Lab at NTUST. I built a GitOps CI/CD pipeline with Jenkins, ArgoCD, and Quay to deploy and test O-RAN software across on-prem OpenShift, StarlingX, and vanilla Kubernetes clusters. I wrote NFO and rApp microservices in Go and Python for O-Cloud infrastructure management and test orchestration, and contributed upstream to O-RAN SC via Gerrit.

Before that, I worked as a System Integrator at the Telecom Infra Project Community Lab in Bandung, Indonesia. I integrated OpenRAN and OpenWiFi hardware, tested cell site routers against DANOS, DentOS, SONiC, and OcNOS, and deployed containerized network functions on Red Hat OpenShift and RHEL/KVM. I also automated TIP VPN and remote access infrastructure, supporting 300+ concurrent users during live training events.

## Focus areas

- Bare-metal provisioning (PXE, TFTP, DHCP) and Ansible
- Network function virtualization and O-RAN
- Go, Python, and Bash tooling

## Education and research

I hold an M.Sc. in Electronics and Computer Engineering from National Taiwan University of Science and Technology and a B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from Telkom University. I've published research on post-quantum cryptography for IoT and energy systems monitoring.
